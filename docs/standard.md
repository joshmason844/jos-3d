# The standard

**Challenge (Josh, 14 Oct 2026): get every page to 8/10.** Runs work on the lowest-scoring page first. Progress is in `docs/progress.md`.

Each run scores its page out of 10 with fresh eyes on every line below. A page is "at standard" when it scores 8+ on two runs in a row. After that it only gets fixes, and the runs move on to the pages that score lowest.

| Check      | 10 looks like                                                                        |
|------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Shape      | Real proportions and sizes; nothing floating, sunk, clipping or poking through        |
| Detail     | Close up, the things you'd notice in real life are there (joints, seams, straps, nails) |
| Surface    | Materials read as what they are: canvas, timber, steel, skin, hair, concrete          |
| Light      | Soft contact shadows, no black blobs, no blown highlights, readable on a phone        |
| Truth      | Matches Josh's photos and facts; anything guessed has the orange "not sure" tag      |
| Works      | Zero page errors, loads on a phone, buttons don't overlap, labels readable           |

Scores go in `docs/scorecard.md` as `Score: Shape x · Detail x · Surface x · Light x · Truth x · Works x = avg`.

## What the numbers mean (so every reviewer marks the same way)
- **4**: obviously computer-made at a glance; blocks, blobs, holes or smears you notice straight away.
- **6**: reads as the right thing from normal distance; falls apart close up or at an angle (plain blocks, soft smears, flat light).
- **8**: holds up close and from any angle on a phone; real materials and contact shadows; you'd only spot it's 3D if you looked for it.
- **10**: could pass for a photo.
Reviewers score BEFORE and AFTER with this scale. Progress uses the AFTER score, but a run only counts as better if AFTER beats BEFORE from the same reviewer.
